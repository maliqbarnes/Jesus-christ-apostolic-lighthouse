Our Mission

A lighthouse for our city. A Kingdom for this generation.

Jesus Christ Apostolic Lighthouse Kingdom Ministries International is a beacon of faith, love, and truth. We exist to empower individuals and families through the Word of God, fervent prayer, and community outreach.

About the Ministry

Serving New Orleans with Apostolic faith and Kingdom purpose.

Founded on the uncompromised Word of God, JCAL Ministries strives to reach souls across Westwego, New Orleans, and beyond. We cultivate a vibrant community where spiritual growth, authentic fellowship, and servant leadership thrive.

Whether you are seeking a spiritual home, looking to deepen your relationship with God, or searching for community, there is a place for you at JCAL.

Apostolic Doctrine

What We Believe

Anchored in the uncompromised Word of God and the Apostolic doctrine.

✝

One God — Jesus Christ

We believe in one God, revealed as Father in creation, Son in redemption, and Holy Spirit in regeneration. We believe that Jesus Christ is God manifested in the flesh, the fullness of the Godhead bodily, and the only saving name given among men.

Scripture Proof:
Deuteronomy 6:4 Isaiah 9:6 John 1:1, 14 John 10:30 Colossians 2:9 1 Timothy 3:16 Acts 4:12
🕊

The New Birth

We believe the biblical response to the Gospel is repentance, water baptism by immersion in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost according to apostolic doctrine.

Scripture Proof:
John 3:3–5 Luke 24:47 Acts 2:38–39 Acts 8:12–17 Acts 10:44–48 Acts 19:1–6
🔥

The Holy Ghost

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Ghost and in the continuing work of the Spirit in the Church. The Holy Ghost empowers believers for holy living, spiritual gifts, spiritual fruit, and bold witness, with speaking in other tongues as the Spirit gives utterance demonstrated in the apostolic church.

Scripture Proof:
Joel 2:28–29 Acts 1:8 Acts 2:1–4 Acts 10:44–46 Acts 19:6 1 Cor 12:4–11 Galatians 5:22–23
👑

Holiness & Christian Living

We believe God's people are called to holiness in heart and conduct. The new life in Christ should produce obedience, love, modesty, self-control, separation from sin, and continual growth into the character of Jesus Christ.

Scripture Proof:
Romans 12:1–2 1 Cor 6:19–20 2 Cor 7:1 Galatians 5:16–25 Hebrews 12:14 1 Peter 1:15–16
🏛

The Church & Ministry

We believe the Church is the body of Christ, called to continue steadfastly in the apostles' doctrine, make disciples, proclaim the Gospel, serve others, demonstrate the power of the Holy Ghost, and equip believers for the work of ministry.

Scripture Proof:
Matthew 28:19–20 Mark 16:15–18 Acts 1:8 Acts 2:42 1 Cor 12:27–28 Ephesians 4:11–13
☁

The Second Coming & Eternity

We believe Jesus Christ will return for His Church. We believe in the resurrection of the dead, final judgment, eternal life for the redeemed, and the fulfillment of God's eternal Kingdom.

Scripture Proof:
John 14:1–3 Acts 1:9–11 1 Cor 15:51–52 1 Thess 4:16–17 Revelation 20:11–15 Revelation 21:1–4
